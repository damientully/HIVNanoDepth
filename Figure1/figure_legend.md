**Figure. Relationship between plasma viral load, sequencing depth, and consensus error rate (n = 68 samples).** (A-C) Distributions of mean sequencing depth, log10-transformed plasma viral load, and weighted ONT consensus error rate; violin plots with overlaid boxplots (median, IQR) and individual sample values (jitter). (D) Weighted error rate versus mean sequencing depth (log scale), with LOESS fit and 95% confidence band (Spearman rho = -0.63, P = 8.3 x 10^-9). (E) Mean sequencing depth versus log10 viral load, with linear fit and 95% confidence band (Pearson r = -0.27, P = 0.028). (F) Weighted error rate versus log10 viral load, with linear fit and 95% confidence band (Pearson r = -0.03, P = 0.83). (G) Pearson correlation matrix of the three variables. A multiple linear regression of error rate on log10(viral load) and mean depth showed depth as the dominant predictor (beta = -6.6 x 10^-7 per read, P = 1.0 x 10^-9) and a small independent contribution of viral load (beta = -0.0025, P = 0.034), together explaining 44% of the variance in error rate (R^2 = 0.44).
